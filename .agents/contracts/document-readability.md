# Review readability and completeness

Strongly prefer one reviewable topic per page, normally readable in five to seven
minutes (roughly 1,400 words). This is an editorial target, not a pass/fail limit.
There is no maximum word, line, source, segment, table-row or aggregate-file count.
Sales YAML may contain many complete segment pages; Research may be a corpus of
an overview, segment audits and competitor detail. Do not count either as one
linear review page or truncate it in loaders, validators, exports or agent output.

Keep the main document self-contained for its decision. Remove repeated prose,
then group long material into named, navigable pages registered in the product
catalog. Retain material facts, numbers/units, segment differences, customer
motives, objections, CJM routes, exceptions, commercial terms, finding IDs,
sources/dates, contrary evidence, confidence and limitations in their owning
pages. An overview summarizes conclusions and points to detail; it never replaces
or silently drops the evidence. If splitting would break necessary context, keep
a longer page. Missing information cannot be justified by the reading-time target.

Use the canonical primary headings and existing shared page browser; additional
pages need no uniform Markdown body schema. Summarization must preserve meaning,
source ownership and traceability. Do not create duplicate facts or an approval
summary. Review status stays in metadata and the header, not in editorial prose.
