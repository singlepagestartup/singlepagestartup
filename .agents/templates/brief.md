# Brief

## Source request

- Origin, date, participants, decision authority
- Raw request and desired outcome

## Founder wording

- Exact descriptions, terms, assumptions, and disputed language

## Business and offer

- Current offer, audience, geography, price or calculation rule
- Current acquisition, sales, delivery, follow-up, and capacity

## Business-model signals

- Primary and secondary value exchange or transaction mechanisms
- Buyer, user, payer, beneficiary, and decision authority when they differ
- Monetization, delivery, cost/capacity, scaling, and regulatory signals
- Preserve uncertainty here; normalization belongs in the decision profile

## Existing evidence and assets

- Client materials, listings, photographs, testimonials, rights, and limitations

## Constraints and goals

- Budget, timing, legal/operational constraints, success criteria, non-goals

## Unknowns and conflicts

- Ordered by the decisions they can change
