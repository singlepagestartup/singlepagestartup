## Model scope

## Revenue Streams

Per product/segment: payer, paid value unit, price or calculation rule, currency,
one-time/recurring basis, payment timing and material commercial conditions.
Explicitly distinguish free access, unknown price, customer revenue and financing.
Store terms once here; Sales references them while keeping its complete process.

## Delivery and resources

### Key Activities

or other existing process; do not summarize that process a second time.

### Key Resources

### Key Partnerships

## Cost Structure

fixed/variable basis, known amount/currency/period and calculation assumptions.
Shared costs are counted once; allocate only with an explicit basis. Refer to
canonical resource IDs and distinguish cash expense from founder time.

### Funding

Revenue Streams. Do not invent budgets, financing or contribution margins.
