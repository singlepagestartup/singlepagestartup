# Evidence register

Use classifications from `.agents/contracts/evidence.md`.

| ID  | Scope | State | Classification | Claim or observation | Source | Accessed | Confidence and limitations | Used by |
| --- | ----- | ----- | -------------- | -------------------- | ------ | -------- | -------------------------- | ------- |

Do not merge client claims, external observations, and inferences into one row.
`Scope` is `singlepage`, `startup`, or `shared`. `State` is `active`,
`not-applicable`, or `superseded`. In a startup project, inherited
`singlepage` rows remain provenance only and cannot support a startup claim
unless a startup row explicitly adopts or supersedes them.
