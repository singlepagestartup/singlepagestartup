## Customer and decision

Identify the audience, user/beneficiary/buyer/payer, decision context and relevant
Sales hypotheses. Explain source population fit and the distinction between
client intent, proposed Sales behavior and observed customers.

## Needs and reasons to choose

| Sales field / hypothesis | Evidence and contrary evidence | Verdict / confidence | Sales and messaging implication |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------ | -------------------- | ------------------------------- |

Cover needs, motivations, purchase_trigger, decision_criteria and objections.
Use stable finding IDs, source links/dates and limitations; avoid unsupported
emotions, personas, success rates or guarantees.

## Acquisition and Customer Journey Map

| Sales acquisition / journey ID | Proposed behavior | Evidence and limitation | Verdict and implication |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |

Inspect each acquisition route and CJM step. Capability or channel availability
alone does not establish discovery, conversion, retention or willingness to pay.

## Commercial conclusions

What is supported, needs refinement, contradicted or unresolved; consequences for
this segment's offer, Sales route, Website and Marketing Creative. Preserve
material uncertainty without turning the page into an engineering test plan.

## Sources

All material sources with stable ID/link, date, publisher, population/sample,
relevance, commercial interest and limitations; no arbitrary source count cap.
