## Measurement scope

Product ID, reporting window, included customer segments and systems inspected.
State which parts of the intended customer journey can and cannot currently be
observed. Missing data is unknown, not zero.

## Funnel observations

Current observations keyed to stable Sales acquisition and journey IDs. Include
the value, denominator where relevant, period, source and data-quality limit.

## Product usage and retention

Observed activation, useful-use, repeat-use, retention, support and abandonment
signals. Keep planned events and instrumentation gaps distinct from measurements.

## Revenue and cost observations

Observed customer revenue and attributable costs for the product and period.
Reference the owning Operations & Economics terms; distinguish funding from
customer revenue and avoid double-counting shared costs.

## Sources and limitations

Name each analytics, CRM, payment, interview or manually inspected source, access
date, coverage, attribution rule and limitation. Research cites this page when it
draws a conclusion; it does not replace the observations with a second copy.
