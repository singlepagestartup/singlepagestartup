# English Unslop

Use with the shared editorial contract. The general pattern catalogue
contains more English examples; this reference addresses syntax and explanation.

## Plain words, complete meaning

Prefer “use”, “check” and “send” to “utilize”, “perform a verification” and
“facilitate delivery”. Name the actor and the object. “Users can continue work
with context” needs the relevant detail: what the service remembers and what
the user can do with it. Add that detail only when the source provides it.

Keep necessary product names, code symbols and technical terms. Explain a term
once where the reader first needs it, then use it consistently. Avoid noun
stacks such as “project knowledge context reuse workflow”; describe the action.

Preserve modal precision: “can” is capability, “may” is possibility or
permission, “must” is a requirement. Do not turn a proposed feature into a
present-tense capability or “up to” into a guaranteed quantity.

## Evidence without lectures

Remove “it's important to note”, “in today's landscape”, inflated adjectives
and unsupported superlatives. State a relevant condition directly rather than
inventing a reader's misconception: “Track visits and completed deployments
separately” is clearer than “A visit doesn't magically become a deployment”.

Keep prices, support commitments, privacy limits and eligibility conditions.
Put an uncertainty beside the affected claim once. Do not append “not a
guarantee” to every paragraph or replace a missing fact with promotional copy.

## Natural structure and tone

Lead with the answer or useful action. Keep subject and verb close. Split a
sentence when several actors or conditions make its meaning hard to follow.
Use paragraphs to develop ideas, not a series of fragments or identical
three-part lists. Passive voice is useful when the actor is irrelevant.

Use contractions where the product's voice allows them. Avoid forced slang,
flattery, fake enthusiasm and manufactured “Question? Answer.” transitions.
Do not make English prose sound like a literal translation of Russian.

At the end, read the result aloud in thought. Can a reader explain the offer,
action and limits without guessing? Check numbers, links, terminology and
promises against the original. Preserve the requested English variety.
