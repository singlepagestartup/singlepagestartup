# Clarity pass for Russian and English

Use after the Unslop pass. Keep the text useful to its intended reader without
assuming specialist knowledge they may not have. Explain the subject with an
adult tone; do not comment on the reader's intelligence.

1. **Point and order.** Start with the answer, event or decision that brought
   the reader here. Each paragraph develops one thought through an explanation,
   example or action. Connect causes to consequences. Remove repeated conclusions.
2. **Actors and actions.** Name who does what, to which object, and under what
   condition. Replace administrative nouns with ordinary verbs. A missing
   object is a missing fact, not a style problem to solve by guessing.
3. **Terms.** Keep a term needed to find a product, setting or document. Explain
   it briefly on first use, then use one name consistently. Expand unfamiliar
   abbreviations. Code symbols and search phrases keep their exact spelling.
4. **Sentences.** Separate tangled actions and conditions. Keep connected prose
   and a natural rhythm. Use lists for steps and tables for comparisons. Do not
   force every paragraph into the same length or sentence pattern.
5. **Evidence.** Preserve amounts, limits, dates that describe events, examples,
   sources and uncertainty. Put conversational provenance in its existing
   metadata. A proposed scenario stays proposed. Never invent a measurement,
   promise, discount, deadline or example presented as a real customer result.
6. **Voice.** Preserve the author's point of view and the product's tone. Remove
   condescension, flattery and lectures about obvious things. State material
   limits beside the relevant claim once; omit repetitive warnings.

## Adapt to the format

- **Workspace document:** help the owner make a decision. Separate the current
  offer, evidence, open questions and next action without repeating the entire
  project context in every section.
- **Article:** answer the reader's question, explain how the approach works,
  and connect the answer to a relevant product action. Keep substantial examples
  and conditions. SEO phrases should read naturally; their inclusion does not
  justify stuffing them into every heading.
- **Landing page:** say who the product is for, what they receive, how to start,
  and what it costs or requires. Use specific examples already in the sources.
  A call to action describes the actual destination or next step.
- **Script or slide:** one spoken or visual thought at a time. Separate narration
  from production directions. Keep enough context for the format to stand alone.
- **UI:** name the action, value or problem. Explain a limit where it affects
  the choice. Never invent a cause for an error or promise when it will be fixed.

Before saving, read the whole text: can the reader understand what happens,
why, and what to do next? Compare facts, links and commitments with the source.
This is an editorial judgment, not a score or evidence of reader testing.
