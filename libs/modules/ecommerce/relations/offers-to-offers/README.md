# Ecommerce Offers to Offers

A directed link connects a retail offer to one of its delivery methods or add-ons. Both offers belong to the same seller. A fulfilled-by link delegates a service to its provider offer for the same Product. A seller fulfills a service directly when it has no provider link.

The source and target are stores-to-products IDs. A source service has at most one fulfiller. The recurrence of a delivery or add-on is first or each, indicating which shipments include its charge.
