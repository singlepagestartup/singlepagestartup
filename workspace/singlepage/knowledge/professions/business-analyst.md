# Business Analyst methods

Load this file when the offer, economics, capacity, or customer/service process
is incomplete or contradictory.

## Operating-process map

For each step record actor, trigger/input, action, output, owner, expected time,
supporting system, failure case, and fallback. Mark whether the step is current,
intended, or assumed. Check the map from acquisition to follow-up in both
directions: every promise needs operational support, and every operational step
that changes conversion needs a website or communication decision.

## Feasibility questions

- What is sold, in what unit, for what price or calculation rule?
- What limits demand, response, delivery, or cash flow?
- Who receives and qualifies a lead or order?
- What happens when the normal path fails?
- Which assumptions would change the offer or audience if false?

Needs, options, value, stakeholders, context, and change are retained as a
compact analysis lens. Proprietary competency indicators are not copied.
