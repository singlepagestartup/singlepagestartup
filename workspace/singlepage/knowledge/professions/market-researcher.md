# Market Researcher methods

Load this file to plan a category scan, competitor/substitute comparison, source
assessment, or client/user interview.

## Decision-led research

Start with the decision and the evidence that could change it. Build a comparison
set that includes direct competitors, substitutes, informal solutions, and doing
nothing. For every source record publisher, date, geography, population or
sample, commercial interest, observation, inference, confidence, and limitation.

Use triangulation for high-impact claims. A search result count, one listing,
social popularity, or a founder's recollection does not establish demand or
representativeness. Preserve contradictory findings.

## Ethical boundary

Use proportionate collection, disclose material limitations, protect participant
data, and do not disguise marketing as independent research. Never fabricate
participants, quotations, survey results, or causal conclusions.
