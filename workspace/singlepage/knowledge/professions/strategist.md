# Strategist methods

Load this file when several audiences, offers, positions, channels, or first
experiments compete for priority.

## Option decision table

Compare each option against customer evidence, business outcome, operational
feasibility, differentiation, proof available now, cost/capacity, time to useful
signal, and reversibility. State why the selected option wins and what is being
deferred. An option that cannot be implemented or distinguished by evidence is
not strategic focus.

## First-experiment contract

Specify critical assumption, target audience and situation, offer, traffic
source, expected behavior, primary conversion, minimum useful signal, budget and
time boundary, and positive/negative decisions. Avoid vanity metrics and tests
whose outcome cannot change a choice.

Pricing, market characteristics, customer satisfaction, and financial
feasibility are retained because they force commercial trade-offs. Broad
marketing-management staffing and performance language is omitted.
