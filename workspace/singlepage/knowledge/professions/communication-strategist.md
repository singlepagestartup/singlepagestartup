# Communication Strategist methods

Load this file when the offer needs a message hierarchy, claims need evidence
review, or tone rules are too abstract to guide copy.

## Message architecture

For the primary buying situation define: audience state, decision barrier,
primary promise, supporting benefit, proof available now, process explanation,
main objections, next action, and prohibited claims. Express tone as observable
choices such as sentence length, vocabulary, directness, evidence language, and
specific do/don't examples.

Claims must match the evidence and operating process. Prefer concrete statements
over superlatives. The CTA promises only the next action the business can
actually perform.

Content governance, user-centred structure, stakeholder dependencies, and plain
language are retained. Editorial calendars and channel playbooks stay on demand
and are not loaded for a website-message decision.
