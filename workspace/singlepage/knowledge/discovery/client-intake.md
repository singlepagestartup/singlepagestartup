# Client intake decisions

Load this reference when `brief.md` is incomplete or the founder has only an
informal description of the business.

## Question order

Ask only questions that can change a later decision. Start with the commercial
exchange and delivery reality, then evidence and constraints:

1. What does the customer buy, why now, and what outcome do they expect?
2. Who buys today, who was expected to buy, and who should not be targeted?
3. How is price formed, what is included, and what creates extra cost or delay?
4. How does a person currently discover, contact, qualify, pay, receive, and
   finish the service? Who owns each handoff?
5. What demand, response, or delivery capacity exists now?
6. Which claims can be demonstrated with listings, messages, invoices, photos,
   cases, reviews, certificates, or other material?
7. Which names, domains, accounts, visual files, photos, testimonials, contact
   data, and tools exist, and who has rights or access?
8. What budget, timing, geography, legal limits, decision authority, and
   non-goals constrain the first result?

Preserve exact founder wording separately from normalized interpretation. End
with unknowns ordered by the downstream decisions they can invalidate.

## Legacy synthesis

The former agency brief's useful business, audience, offer, economics, current
channels, assets, constraints, and creative-context questions are retained.
Preset funnel stages, five-channel planning, long placeholder questionnaires,
and early message/style decisions are omitted because the four-stage workflow
assigns them to later evidence-aware owners.
