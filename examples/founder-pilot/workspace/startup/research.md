# Research — Moscow wooden-window repair pilot

## Decision questions and method

Decisions: which first buying situation to serve, which proof and price information visitors expect, and which acquisition surface can test a photo-based request. Sources are a convenience sample of current competitor/service pages and official Yandex product guidance accessed 2026-08-04. No interviews, keyword-volume data, market sizing, or causal performance evidence were available.

## Audience and purchase situations

- Observable category triggers include worn coating, damaged wood, draught, misaligned hardware, and uncertainty between repair and replacement (`S-01`–`S-03`).
- Customers need to judge fit, likely scope, trust, process, proof, and cost before inviting a master into the home. This is an inference from competitor page structure, not a measured priority order.
- Primary provisional situation: an owner of a wooden window in a Moscow apartment sees deterioration but wants to understand whether it can reasonably be retained (`A-01`).
- Alternatives: do nothing; repaint or seal it personally; hire a marketplace handyman; use a specialized repair company; replace the window; ask an architect/restoration specialist for sensitive buildings.

## Market and competition

- Specialist sites commonly show before/after examples, staged assessment, warranty or contract language, and photo-based calculation (`S-03`). The pilot lacks the evidence to repeat those claims.
- Published prices vary by unit and scope: one competitor lists 8,000 ₽/m² for restoration/painting while a marketplace lists task-level ranges (`S-01`, `S-02`). These are anchors to explain why a single unsupported price would mislead, not a pricing recommendation.
- Marketplace supply and ratings make “professional quality” non-distinctive. A sharper decision is a calm, personal repairability assessment with explicit limits (`A-01`, `P-02`).
- Yandex Business can display services/prices and favors real service photos; its form/gallery capabilities make it a plausible later source for a bounded local test (`S-04`, `S-05`). Eligibility and economics still require account-level confirmation.

## Findings

| Finding                                                                                            | Evidence IDs | Type                       | Confidence       | Decision impact                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ | -------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Visitors will encounter broad price ranges and multiple provider formats.                          | S-01, S-02   | Observation                | Medium           | Explain calculation factors; do not invent a starting price.                  |
| Real work imagery is a category trust device and a platform expectation.                           | S-03, S-04   | Observation                | Medium           | Launch paid traffic only after rights-cleared photos exist.                   |
| “Send photos for an assessment” is established category behavior.                                  | S-03         | Observation                | Medium           | A photo-first CTA is understandable, but needs a precise operational promise. |
| Preservation-first personal assessment can focus the pilot without claiming technical superiority. | A-01, P-02   | Inference/decision         | Low until tested | Use it as positioning, with a replacement caveat.                             |
| A Yandex surface could reach people already looking for a local service.                           | S-05         | Observation plus inference | Medium/low       | Candidate for one later experiment, not a launch commitment.                  |

## Contradictions and unknowns

- A preservation message may attract protected/heritage work beyond founder competence; the first scope explicitly excludes it.
- Competitors can publish warranty, experience, portfolios, and free visits; the pilot cannot until `M-01` and `U-01` are resolved.
- No evidence shows the number, acquisition cost, or conversion propensity of the provisional audience. Strategy must cap spend and optimize for learning.
