# Business — «Рама»

## Business model

- Customer problem: an owner sees draught, sticking hardware, damaged coating, cracks, or worn wood but cannot tell whether repair is sensible.
- Value exchange: founder evaluates the case, explains viable scope, and performs agreed repair/restoration while preserving the window when practical.
- Offer unit: diagnostic visit plus a scoped work estimate; repair is priced by condition, dimensions, access, materials, and selected finish.
- Revenue: inspection where applicable and completed work. Costs, minimum order, margin, travel radius, and capacity remain `U-01` and must be confirmed before publication.
- Intended first outcome: fewer incomplete enquiries and a repeatable path from photo request to accepted scope.

## Lead, payment, and delivery rules

- Lead receiver: founder. Required data: name, reachable phone, Moscow district, short problem description, window photos, consent.
- Qualification: wooden residential window, reachable service area, assessable problem, and scope compatible with founder capacity. Final rule waits on `U-01`.
- Response promise: `P-01` only after founder confirmation. Acknowledgement must not imply acceptance.
- Pricing: no public “from” price copied from competitors. Explain calculation factors; quote only after evidence appropriate to the scope.
- Payment, deposit, warranty, cancellation, legal documents, and service radius remain blocking unknowns.

## Promises and constraints

- Supported direction: personal review and an explanation of whether inspection is worthwhile (`C-02`, `P-02`).
- Prohibited: guaranteed restoration, remote diagnosis, cheapest/best claims, heritage expertise, fixed price, a team, large portfolio, or quantified experience.
- If capacity or response time changes, update this file first; `strategy.md`, `brand.md`, and `website.md` become review targets through reverse dependencies.
