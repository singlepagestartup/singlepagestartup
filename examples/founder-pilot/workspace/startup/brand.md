# Brand — «Рама»

## Communication

- Brand name: `Рама` — a pilot naming decision, subject to legal/domain checks before adoption.
- Audience state: “С окном что-то не так; я не понимаю, ремонтировать его или менять.”
- Primary message: first understand the condition, then decide the scope.
- Supporting promise: “Алексей лично посмотрит заявку и ответит в следующий рабочий день” (`P-01`, publish only after confirmation).
- Proof hierarchy now: transparent assessment process → explicit limits → founder accountability. Later: rights-cleared before/after work and permissioned reviews (`M-01`).
- Objections: uncertain repairability, price, dust/access, timing, and trust. Answer concretely; do not hide unknown price behind “выгодно”.
- Primary CTA: `Отправить фото на оценку`. Secondary CTA: `Посмотреть, как проходит работа`.
- Voice: calm, direct, technically careful, first-person accountability. Use short verbs and say what happens next.
- Avoid: “премиум”, “эксперт №1”, “любая сложность”, “лучшая цена”, artificial urgency, faux heritage language, or plural “мы”.

## Identity direction

- Idea: a joiner's registration mark — measured, warm, and repair-oriented rather than nostalgic luxury.
- Character: attentive, practical, quiet, honest about uncertainty.
- Selected territory: dark green structure, warm clay action, paper background, thin measured lines, generous spacing.
- Rejected: glossy construction-company blue, pseudo-imperial ornament, rustic craft clichés, and generated workshop photography.

## Normative system

- Use green for structure and clay for one primary action; never use color alone to signal state.
- Headlines are sentence case with a restrained serif; body and controls use the sans stack at 16px minimum.
- Cards use one-pixel lines, paper surfaces, and functional labels. Buttons have visible focus, 44px minimum target, and explicit text.
- Imagery is either rights-cleared client work labeled by stage or abstract line/vector identity labeled decorative. Never blend the two.
- Components: evidence chip, process step, scope card, before/after placeholder, photo-request form, success panel.
- Do show condition, process, and limits. Do not imply a workshop, team, tools, scale, protected-object competence, or completed projects.
