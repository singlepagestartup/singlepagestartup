# Strategy — first commercial choice

## Commercial choice

- Primary audience: Moscow apartment owners with deteriorating wooden windows who prefer to assess repair before replacement (`A-01`).
- Buying situation: visible wear, draught, sticking hardware, or uncertainty about repairability; commercial and protected-heritage projects are excluded from the first offer.
- Positioning: a personal, photo-first assessment that explains the next sensible step without promising restoration before inspection (`C-02`, `P-02`).
- Offer: send the problem, district, and photos; the founder replies with clarification, a likely path, or a recommendation for inspection (`P-01`, pending capacity confirmation).
- Proof available now: transparent process and sourced category context. Missing: publishable work, reviews, warranty, price, legal identity, capacity (`M-01`, `U-01`).
- Main objections: “Can this be repaired?”, “How much?”, “Can I trust one master?”, “Will the work damage the apartment?”, “When will I hear back?”
- Acquisition focus: first complete the page and obtain rights-cleared real photos; then test one local intent surface, not multiple channels.
- Non-goals: heritage authority, lowest price, instant quote, nationwide coverage, a large company image, automated booking, or production marketing execution.

## First experiment

This is an experiment contract, not a campaign already launched.

| Field                                    | Decision                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Critical assumption                      | Owners in the selected situation will submit photos and enough context for a useful assessment.                                                       |
| Preconditions                            | Founder confirms capacity/SLA/legal wording; at least six rights-cleared real photos exist; final phone and service radius are known.                 |
| Audience and offer                       | Moscow residential owners; photo-first repairability assessment, no instant price.                                                                    |
| Traffic source                           | One Yandex local service placement or profile link, selected only after eligibility/account review (`S-05`).                                          |
| Expected behavior and primary conversion | Visitor reviews fit/process and submits a complete request with photos.                                                                               |
| Minimum useful signal                    | 30 relevant landing visits and at least 3 complete requests within 14 days; treat this as a learning threshold, not statistical proof.                |
| Budget/time limit                        | Maximum 15,000 ₽ and 14 days; pause sooner if capacity is filled or irrelevant requests exceed 70%.                                                   |
| Positive decision                        | At least 3 complete requests, at least 2 operationally qualified, and founder can respond within the promised window: iterate copy and qualification. |
| Negative decision                        | Fewer than 2 complete requests after 30 relevant visits: inspect traffic intent, missing proof, form friction, and offer before more spend.           |
| Stop rule                                | Stop immediately if the promise cannot be fulfilled, consent is invalid, or traffic attracts excluded heritage/commercial work.                       |

## Risks and missing evidence

- The numeric thresholds and budget are bounded planning assumptions, not forecasts.
- Without genuine imagery, paid acquisition should not start (`S-04`, `M-01`). Decorative pilot vectors must not substitute for proof (`K-01`).
- If founder capacity makes `P-01` impossible, change `business.md` first and recompute dependent communication and page decisions.
