- `bridge`: AI Chat can support framework evaluation. Research tests the connection; service usage/payment alone does not demonstrate framework adoption.
- `capacity`: delivery and best-effort help can fit the shared founder limit. Sales exposes unresolved owners and failures; no support timing commitment may be inferred.
- `service-viability`: customer problem, payer, value unit, price, eligibility and operating terms remain open. They block paid/external service decisions, not recording known framework facts.
- External availability, privacy/retention, AI authority, day reset, reconciliation, incident/security and refund/reversal ownership need resolution in AI Chat Product/Sales before external use. Market research cannot decide these operator facts.
- No supplied independent proof establishes production readiness, compatibility, security, easy setup, time saved, integration, successful use or adoption. Relevant findings and contradictions remain in each product's Research.
