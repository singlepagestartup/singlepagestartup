# Competitors and alternatives

## Comparison scope

The relevant decision is how a person turns incomplete business material into an approved project structure and then uses that context for reasoned discussion, criticism and useful next materials. The comparison therefore includes persistent AI workspaces, business-knowledge agents, separate planning and website tools, professional help, informal notes and doing nothing.

This page compares documented offers and workflows. It does not report tested answer quality, customer preference or migration outcomes. Prices, availability and terms require a fresh check before a commercial decision.

## Persistent general AI workspaces

**AC-SPS-20 — documented alternative, high confidence in capability.** ChatGPT Projects keeps chats, files and instructions together and can use connected sources. A project owner can upload material, provide project instructions and continue across chats. This overlaps with AI Chat's persistent project context. The current comparison does not establish whether the SinglePageStartup decision structure produces better results or less work. [S1]

**AC-SPS-25 — documented capacity limit, medium confidence in the exact figure.** ChatGPT Projects caps the files a project may hold, per plan and in the low tens: the figures OpenAI publishes are 5 for Free, 25 for Go and Plus and 40 for Pro, Business, Education and Enterprise, and its own pages disagree, with the File Uploads FAQ giving 20 where the Projects article gives 25. A corpus of hundreds of documents is therefore outside the product's design, whatever the exact number. The coordinator could not open the official pages directly, which is why the figure is recorded as approximate and the order of magnitude as the reliable part. [S3]

Appeal: familiar general-purpose assistance, broad tasks and an existing ecosystem. Limitation for this decision: project organization and progression to business-model, Sales and material pages depend on the user's instructions and chosen workflow, and the amount of material a project can hold is bounded.

## Business-knowledge agents

**AC-SPS-21 — documented adjacent alternative, high confidence in capability.** Chatbase accepts documents, text and websites as data sources for an agent. This overlaps with business-specific knowledge and repeated answers. It focuses on configuring and deploying an agent rather than the proposed Request → Strategy → Brand → Products workspace. Documented differences are product scope, not proof of superior customer value. [S2]

Appeal: a defined knowledge-agent product and deployment paths. Limitation for this decision: it does not by itself establish the complete project-planning and first-page workflow proposed for AI Chat.

## Separate documents and website tools

**AC-SPS-22 — substitute, medium confidence.** A project owner can write a brief, strategy and brand in separate documents, then use a website builder or another agent to create a landing page. This may be sufficient when the person already has a method or needs only one deliverable. The cost is potential repeated transfer and manual synchronization; the current research has not measured that cost for the segment.

Appeal: independent best-of-category tools, familiar formats and no commitment to one workspace. Limitation: connections, ownership and change impact may depend on the user maintaining them.

## Consultants, agencies and collaborators

**AC-SPS-23 — service alternative, medium confidence.** A founder can hire professional help or work with a team member who elicits the request, proposes a direction and prepares materials. Human judgment, accountability and facilitation may be preferable for high-stakes or ambiguous work. AI Chat should not represent a guided card flow as a replacement for professional advice or delegated responsibility.

Appeal: tailored judgment and shared responsibility. Limitation: availability, cost and handoff depend on the provider and engagement.

## Informal work and doing nothing

**AC-SPS-24 — status-quo alternatives, high confidence in relevance.** Notes, chat history, spreadsheets, saved messages and memory may be enough for an early idea. The person can also postpone structured work until the need becomes urgent. These alternatives have little setup cost and should remain in comparisons.

Appeal: immediate, flexible and familiar. Limitation: important decisions may remain implicit, scattered or difficult to reuse. This is a plausible problem statement, not a measured frequency or financial loss.
