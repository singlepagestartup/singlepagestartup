# Early-stage project owners: Sales assumptions and evidence

## Scope and verdict rules

This page audits Sales segment `business-users`: people with an idea or early-stage project who want their existing material structured for approval, a context-aware counterpart for later thinking, a landing-page sandbox and continued work without repeatedly supplying the same context. It checks the proposed profile, all three acquisition routes and every Customer Journey Map step.

`Supported` means an attributable source supports the claim in its stated setting. `Partially supported` means only an adjacent need or population is supported. `Unresolved` means the current review cannot determine customer behavior. Client intentions and professional proposals remain valid inputs but are not market proof.

## Needs, motivations and purchase trigger

**AC-SPS-11 — partially supported, medium confidence.** Project tools and business-knowledge assistants show that people can retain files and instructions across related chats or configure knowledge from business material. This supports the capability to accept existing material and avoid repeatedly supplying context. It does not establish that users want AI Chat to attribute mixed input to the compact Request → Strategy → Brand → Design → Products structure, nor that they will trust the proposed attribution. The Sales need remains a product hypothesis. [AI Chat Research AC-SPS-02](../research.md#alternatives-and-competition)

**AC-SPS-12 — unresolved, low confidence.** Turning available material into a reviewable foundation and later using it for reasoned idea work are plausible motivations. Current sources contain no direct AI Chat customer interviews, completion data or comparison with a general assistant and separate documents. Demonstrate both approaches with the same material and idea question; record attribution corrections, objections raised, abandonment and perceived usefulness before making a speed, simplicity or thinking-quality claim.

**AC-SPS-13 — unresolved, low confidence.** A landing-page sandbox followed by guided publication is the selected next result, but no evidence establishes that this is the most valuable continuation for the segment. Observe whether users edit the sandbox, authorize GitHub, create the repository, connect a server, complete deployment, choose another project decision or stop after accepting the project page.

**AC-SPS-14 — unresolved, low confidence.** The proposed purchase trigger is useful initial work followed by a need for more. No product-specific willingness-to-pay, repeat-use or token-package evidence exists. Keep initial usefulness, return use and purchase separate in measurement.

## Resulting Sales and material decisions

The Sales structure is coherent enough for review. Access, database-backed sandbox behavior, guided GitHub publication and the initial support promise are now defined as operator intentions rather than market evidence. Readiness remains blocked by the token offer, exact legal/payment details, named-provider disclosures and unresolved repository/server integration terms. Website and Promotion materials may show the proposed workflow and distinguish it from alternatives. They must avoid claims of faster launch, better answers, reduced cost, demand or retained use until those outcomes are observed.

The next research should use the actual Website layouts and Product Content card definitions with suitable project owners. Preserve the person's wording, task, corrections and chosen continuation. A favorable walkthrough demonstrates that case; it does not establish the market as a whole.
