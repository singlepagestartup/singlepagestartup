# Early-stage project owners: Sales assumptions and evidence

## Scope and verdict rules

This page audits Sales segment `business-users`: people with an idea or early-stage project who want editable descriptions drafted from their material, to correct shortcomings and to reuse or export the accepted text. The first-stage structure has project-wide Brief, Strategy, Brand and written Design, plus each product's own customer and offer, Operations & Economics, Sales, Promotion, Analytics and applicable research. Promotion is the written description of public presentation, including Website, Creative and Presentation text where relevant. The audit checks the proposed profile, all three acquisition routes and every current Customer Journey Map step. Landing-page assembly and server deployment are deferred.

`Supported` means an attributable source supports the claim in its stated setting. `Partially supported` means only an adjacent need or population is supported. `Unresolved` means the current review cannot determine customer behavior. Client intentions and professional proposals remain valid inputs but are not market proof.

## Needs, motivations and purchase trigger

**AC-SPS-11 — partially supported, medium confidence.** Project tools and business-knowledge assistants document retention of files and instructions across related chats or knowledge configured from business material. This supports the capability to accept existing material and reuse context. It does not establish that users want AI Chat's shortened Brief, Strategy, Brand, written Design and separate product documents, nor that the proposed attribution and per-product section coverage are useful. The Sales need remains a product hypothesis. [AI Chat Research AC-SPS-02](../research.md#alternatives-and-competition)

**AC-SPS-12 — unresolved, low confidence.** Turning available material into usable descriptions, correcting shortcomings and using the accepted text inside or outside AI Chat are plausible motivations. Current sources contain no direct AI Chat customer interviews, completion data or comparison with a general assistant and separate documents. Compare the same material and later idea question. Record each product's section coverage, attribution corrections, remaining contradictions, export, abandonment and perceived usefulness before making a speed, simplicity or thinking-quality claim.

**AC-SPS-13 — deferred hypothesis, unresolved, low confidence.** Landing-page assembly, editing and paid server deployment belong to future development after evaluation and improvement of written project description. No evidence establishes preference for that continuation or demand for a deployment offer. When that offer is in scope, distinguish page review, publication, initial enquiries and hypothesis learning. GitHub authorization, repository creation and server connection are then separate process observations. None is a first-stage activation or completion condition. [Brief](../../../../brief/singlepage.md#project-and-products)

**AC-SPS-14 — unresolved, low confidence.** The proposed reason to pay is useful initial work followed by a need to continue. [Sales](../sales.yaml) defines the immediate trigger as reaching the negative-balance limit or choosing to top up earlier. No product-specific willingness-to-pay, repeat-use or token-package evidence exists. Keep initial usefulness, return use, purchase and resumption of an interrupted task separate in measurement.

## Resulting Sales and material decisions

The first-stage offer asks users to describe their projects, review shortened documents and correct shortcomings the AI agent identifies. Access, saved descriptions and the initial support promise are operator intentions, not market evidence. [Product](../product.md) defines token and provider terms; [Sales](../sales.yaml) has no open first-stage operator-fact blockers. Failed or cancelled AI requests receive a full token return under Product; this policy is not evidence of customer comprehension or implemented billing. [Website](../website/new-project.md#how-your-materials-are-processed-and-stored) defines processing, storage and deletion disclosure. Website and Promotion materials may demonstrate the document workflow and its correction paths. Claims of faster launch, superior answers, demand or retained use require observed outcomes. Future deployment prices remain outside this first-stage review.

The next research should use the actual intake and document-review layouts with suitable project owners. Preserve the person's wording, task, per-product section coverage, corrections, export attempts and chosen continuation. Compare accepted descriptions with what the person needs to do next. A favorable walkthrough demonstrates that case; it does not establish the market as a whole. AC-SPS-13 stays deferred; publication metrics are not current review criteria.
