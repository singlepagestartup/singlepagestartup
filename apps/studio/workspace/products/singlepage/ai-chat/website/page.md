# Turn your notes and drafts into a business model and prepare your first landing page.

Business model and landing-page sandbox

Upload notes, drafts, documents and images. AI Chat organizes them into a business model, shows what is missing and assembles a landing-page preview from SinglePageStartup blocks.

[Organize my materials](/register)

[Sign in](/login)

Bring the material in its current form. You do not need to decide where every item belongs before uploading it.

<!-- section: foundation -->

## See the whole business and what is still missing.

AI Chat uses the SinglePageStartup method, developed through long-term work with business, marketing and product-development methods and adapted for AI agents. It places the supplied material into working sections that you can edit, discuss and export.

| Working area           | What it gives you                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Business foundation    | The request, intended result, boundaries and important missing decisions.                   |
| Products and customers | Customer segments, their problems, the offer and the value each product creates.            |
| Operations and sales   | How the business delivers value, earns revenue, pays its costs and works with customers.    |
| Marketing system       | Strategy, brand, website, materials and a landing page that can be previewed and published. |

<!-- section: workflow -->

## Upload, complete and inspect the first page.

| Step                   | What happens                                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Upload the material    | Add notes, documents, screenshots, images and an explanation in their current form.        |
| Fill important gaps    | Answer only the questions needed to complete the current business decision.                |
| Work with the model    | Correct sections, ask for criticism, compare alternatives and export the current document. |
| Prepare the first page | Turn the selected customer, offer and action into an editable landing-page sandbox.        |

<!-- section: publish -->

## Preview the landing page, then publish it on your server.

Use the customer, problem, offer and action already defined in the workspace. Edit the Text and Layout built from SinglePageStartup blocks. Changes are saved with the project and shown immediately in the private frontend preview.

[Open the landing-page sandbox](/projects/example/landing-page)

When the page is ready, sign in with GitHub and create the project repository. Connect an existing server or a hosting provider such as Beget or Timeweb, enter its deployment key and publish the site.

[Publish on my server](/projects/example/landing-page/publish)

Ask about an idea, request criticism, update a document or prepare the next material using the business already described in the workspace.
