# Product

## Product identity

| Decision          | Intended product                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Brand and product | **AI Chat** (`ai-chat`) is SinglePageStartup's central intended hosted workspace and a working demonstration of its framework.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Category          | Guided AI business workspace that turns existing files and answers into shortened, editable project and product documents.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Lifecycle         | Intended product developed under the project owner's direction.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Boundary          | The first-stage offer includes file and image intake, guided questions, written project and product descriptions, project-aware chat, export and hosted token use. ## Customer Segments

| Role           | Known situation and responsibility                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| User           | `business-users`: people with a business idea or early-stage project who need to turn available files, notes and images into a usable project description, see what is missing and take a first step toward customers. A narrower industry is not selected. |
| Buyer          | A user choosing to buy tokens to continue paid AI work when the debt limit blocks new requests, or topping up earlier; an organization's separate purchasing role is unspecified.                                                                           |
| Payer          | The person or organization funding those purchases; payer identity and purchasing authority remain commercial decisions.                                                                                                                                    |
| Beneficiary    | The user and the project or business being described, tested and developed.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Decision-maker | The user reviews and approves the project and product descriptions, corrects shortcomings and chooses later work; a future organization's internal purchasing authority remains customer-specific.                                                          |

People who need the hosted workspace can remain AI Chat users. A separate concrete software need can lead them to the existing Code Framework; server publication through AI Chat belongs to the future offer.

The qualifying need is a project whose scattered material and unsettled decisions must become a maintained business model, rather than a single isolated answer. The proposed segment excludes commissioned custom website work, continuous live assistance and guaranteed business results: none belongs to the current offer. This is a professional-choice boundary based on the Brief and the support and responsibility limits below, not an observed customer preference.

## Value Propositions

The user names a project, uploads notes, documents, screenshots and images on the material screen and follows the analysis. Each prepared document then opens as a chat for discussion and editing. AI Chat keeps the framework's shortened Workspace structure: Brief, Strategy, Brand, Design and Products. Brief supplies the project brief; Strategy and Brand describe the direction and meaning; Design describes the intended appearance in words. Each product has its own Product/customer/offer, Operations & Economics, Sales, Promotion, Analytics and research or evidence where applicable. Promotion is a short written description of the intended public presentation, with Website, Creative and Presentation text where relevant; it does not deliver finished visual assets or a built landing page. Compactness reduces the initial detail without combining all products into one shared offer or removing their decisions.

The first chat session is structured to produce a reviewed project model in about one focused hour. A person can leave an answer unknown, return later or open the relevant detailed section when a decision, process or material needs more work.

The model is assembled through the SinglePageStartup method, which the owner built by combining and adapting business, marketing and product-development practice. Its structure, its ownership of decisions and the links between sections exist so an agent can work in it: place a fact where it belongs, find a gap or a contradiction, correct the owning section and reuse an accepted decision later.

Each generated file is a working thread. The person and AI agent discuss and edit Brief, Strategy, Brand, Design and Products separately, preserving each file's conversation and edits. The general project workspace holds additional discussion topics; the person chooses which saved documents to attach as context for each topic. They can develop an idea, ask for criticism, weigh alternatives or prepare a material using that context, with questions for what the current decision still needs. Reviewed documents remain available for export.

The AI agent identifies shortcomings and contradictions for the user to correct. The resulting description is a first step toward customers; it does not establish demand or guarantee a market result. The owner evaluates whether this description workflow works for the first users and improves it from their work and feedback.

General AI chats without a maintained project structure and separate planning documents are relevant alternatives for the first-stage offer. Current [Research](research.md) compares business-context chat alternatives; preference for the guided project-description workflow remains unproven. More useful written work and less repeated explanation are intended outcomes, not established advantages.

## Offer and usage

Access provides the hosted workspace and this intended sequence:

1. Register with email and password and receive the currently available free token allowance.
2. Create a project by entering its name. Upload notes, documents, photographs and images on the next screen, with an optional description. Follow the analysis on its own screen and review the facts AI Chat extracts from the material.
3. Read the agent's questions and identified gaps. Answer while it drafts compact Brief, Strategy, Brand, Design and Products files, with each product's own Product/customer/offer, Operations & Economics, Sales, Promotion, Analytics and applicable research.
4. Open each file's working thread to discuss and edit it with the agent. Check the proposed changes, correct shortcomings, keep unknowns and conflicts visible and accept the descriptions when ready. Each file retains its own conversation and edits.
5. Create discussion topics in the general project workspace and attach selected saved documents as their knowledge base. Use them to develop or challenge ideas and improve business materials; return to the owning file's thread for document changes. Export the text artifacts for use outside the service.
6. Buy tokens when the balance blocks further paid AI work, or top up earlier; return to the exact saved task after payment.

Uploaded files and the project and product documents are visible alongside the conversations. Each section indicates the material available, what is missing and whether it still needs review. A document's reviewed version stays available as topic context while a new draft is being revised; the new text replaces it after review. The person can continue with selected saved documents while other sections still have open questions. These indicators show the current work and unanswered questions; they do not prove the project's viability.

Registration is by email and password; Google and other social sign-in are not planned. Uploaded documents are split into chunks, vectorized and retrieved as context for each answer, with no limit on how many a person adds. Brand keeps that mechanism out of the ordinary customer explanation.

Future landing-page development uses the accepted descriptions to assemble a page from SinglePageStartup blocks, edit it in a private sandbox and preview saved changes. Its purpose is to collect initial enquiries and test a hypothesis; neither result is guaranteed. The intended later publication route authorizes GitHub for the single repository it creates in the person's account, accepts a deployment key for Beget or Timeweb and deploys through GitHub Actions, including redeployment. A customer-owned domain is attached through the provider's API. These capabilities are outside the first-stage offer; Revenue Streams below owns the unresolved future deployment terms.

The balance is visible during intake and later work. When the debt limit blocks a new paid AI request, the current task is preserved, and the person picks a package, pays and returns to it once the balance permits further requests. Revenue Streams below owns the money terms.

A request sends the data it needs to the selected AI provider under that provider's terms. Deleting an account removes the account and the project data the service controls, while the host's backups keep copies for up to six months, which the project cannot shorten.

The service processes supplied material for the customer's requested work and does not independently analyse or reuse it (`client-claim`; Brief `sources.ai_providers`). This service-use boundary does not establish the AI provider's own handling or training terms.

Customers can freely export and use their material and generated documents or pages outside AI Chat. This permission does not assign copyright ownership or establish rights in third-party inputs. It does not grant independent service reuse or establish the AI provider's own terms.

[Sales](sales.yaml) owns the whole customer process from discovery to continued use. The owner runs every service stage alone and processes support requests within 24 hours as the initial service level, without continuous live coverage. AI Chat does not act on behalf of the business or guarantee market results.

## Revenue Streams

| Element        | Terms                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Payer and unit | The person using the chat buys tokens. One internal token is USD 0.001 of provider cost.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Price          | Sold at a thirty per cent margin over that cost, converted at a fixed 100 roubles per dollar, so a token costs 0.13 roubles.                                                                                                                       |
| Packages       | Five top-ups: 100, 300, 500, 1,000 and 3,000 roubles, at the same token price, so a larger package carries no discount.                                                                                                                            |
| Payment timing | One token is charged when the input is sent to the model; the balance is settled once the answer returns and its cost is known. A successful request costs at least one token, or 0.13 roubles. Failed or cancelled requests have zero net charge. |
| Conditions     | Tokens do not expire. Purchases are normally final; a refund is a case-by-case decision, not a published entitlement. Registration grants the current free allowance.                                                                              |
| Collection     | Payments are accepted in Russia through an online cash register. The accepting party and its details are configured in the product interface, not decided here.                                                                                    |

The margin is taken at top-up and not again on consumption, which is charged at
cost. Settlement rounds each successful turn up to a whole token, which adds an unmeasured
surplus, largest on cheap models. The fixed 100-rouble rate holds the margin only
while the market rate stays at or below it. No revenue or paying-customer
forecast is set.

The balance may become negative. The debt threshold is USD 2 of provider-cost value: USD 2 ÷ USD 0.001 per internal token = 2,000 internal tokens. At a balance of −2,000 internal tokens or below, new paid AI requests are blocked until a top-up raises it above that threshold. The final cost is known only after an answer, so this rule does not promise an in-flight stop or a hard cap on the cost of an already submitted request. Saved work remains available.

A failed or cancelled AI request returns every token deducted for that request to the balance, including the initial token and any settlement. The customer bears no request cost even when the provider has incurred an expense; that expense remains a service cost. This full token reversal is separate from the case-by-case cash-refund policy for purchased packages.

Future server-deployment prices and commercial conditions are undecided (`unknown`; `operator-fact`). They block a deployment offer, not creation and sale of the first-stage text artifacts. The token terms above do not establish a free deployment service.

## Key Activities

Develop and operate the hosted chat: intake of the customer's material,
shortened project and product documents, retrieval over the uploaded corpus and
continued work from accepted descriptions. The owner evaluates first-user work
and corrects shortcomings in the description workflow. Process
support requests within 24 hours as the initial service level, without continuous
live coverage or a resolution deadline. The intended customer process is in
[Sales](sales.yaml); it is not repeated here.

### Funding

The owner's time and money cover the service until its receipts do. Revenue from
this product is what makes the free framework affordable, and the two are kept
apart so neither hides the other. No external financing is committed.
