## Customer Segments

The intended user interacts with an AI agent. The qualifying segment, concrete problem and purchase trigger remain unselected. Buyer, payer, beneficiary and customer decision-maker are explicit unknowns; do not assume these roles coincide. Developer interest in the framework cannot substitute for evidence of demand for this service.

## Problem and desired progress

The intended interaction is a question followed by an agent answer, a bounded refusal, or a recorded failure. The customer outcome that makes this interaction useful, its acceptance condition, and the repeat-use trigger require definition. Speed, accuracy, savings, and autonomous task completion are not established promises.

## Value Propositions

The current defensible description is **AI Chat — a service for interacting with AI agents**. A differentiated offer and a service-specific competitive comparison remain open. Demonstrating framework capabilities is an internal business purpose; it is not sufficient customer value by itself.

## Offer and usage

The usage unit is an agent interaction; its useful result and acceptance condition remain to be defined. Access requires established eligibility and boundaries. The complete registration, access, interaction and follow-up process is in [Sales](sales.yaml), including refusal, failure, recovery and handoffs.

Possible token or paid-chat mechanics are not an approved price list. [Operations & Economics](../models/framework-service/model.md#cost-structure) owns the promotional cost limits; they do not define paid access. Founder support is best effort through messenger-linked CRM visibility, without a response-time promise.

## Evidence and decision rules

Client scope and experiment decisions supply the intended mechanics and separate product identity (sources: scope/intention). No independent external successful-use or repeat-use result has been supplied. Service payment or engagement does not establish Code Framework adoption.

The next product decision is the intended user's concrete problem and the bounded service offer. Keep paid access and availability claims unpublished until terms and delivery controls are confirmed.
