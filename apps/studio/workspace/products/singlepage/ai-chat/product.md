# AI Chat

## Product identity

| Decision          | Current definition                                                                                                                                                                        |

## Best-fit customer

The intended user chooses an agent and asks a question. A specific problem, qualifying segment, buyer, payer, and purchase trigger have not been selected. Developer interest in the framework cannot substitute for evidence of demand for this service.

## Problem and desired progress

The intended interaction is a question followed by an agent answer, a bounded refusal, or a recorded failure. The customer outcome that makes this interaction useful, its acceptance condition, and the repeat-use trigger require definition. Speed, accuracy, savings, and autonomous task completion are not established promises.

## Positioning and value

The current defensible description is **AI Chat — a service for interacting with AI agents**. A differentiated offer and a service-specific competitive comparison remain open. Demonstrating framework capabilities is an internal business purpose; it is not sufficient customer value by itself.

## Offer and usage

Intended flow: registration → eligible promotional access or a later separately defined purchase → agent choice → question → answer/refusal/failure → repeat use, support, or exit.

Possible token or paid-chat mechanics are not an approved price list. The prior experiment caps promotional provider cost at USD 1 per user/day and USD 200 aggregate; those limits do not define paid access. Founder support is best effort through messenger-linked CRM visibility, without a response-time promise.

Unclear eligibility, cost, capacity, or authority pauses access. Failed interactions must preserve unaffected balances and reconcile affected units. Privacy, retention, AI authority, reset rules, refund/reversal handling and operational owners remain unresolved before external use.

## Evidence and decision rules

Client scope and experiment decisions from 2026-08-09–10 supply the intended mechanics; the client confirmed separate product identity on 2026-09-11. No independent external successful-use or repeat-use result has been supplied. Service payment or engagement does not establish Code Framework adoption.

The next product decision is the intended user's concrete problem and the bounded service offer. Keep paid access and availability claims unpublished until terms and delivery controls are confirmed.
