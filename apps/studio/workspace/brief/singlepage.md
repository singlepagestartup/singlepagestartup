## Business and offer

- **Offer:** free evaluation and use of Code Framework by SinglePageStartup; the framework currently earns no revenue.
- **Value hypothesis:** avoid rebuilding common foundations such as accounts, payments, publishing, profiles, messaging, CRM, AI, and MCP when several are needed in one product.
- **Price and funding:** framework price is zero; founder time and money fund development. No time, cost, reliability, or speed benefit is yet proven.
- **Distribution:** GitHub repository and releases. The intended license is MIT, but the current published license remains restrictive until engineering applies and publishes the change. SP-EV-044.
- **Acquisition:** one founder-led YouTube walkthrough, the demonstration-service bridge, direct preflight with at most two relevant contacts, and GitHub as the evaluation destination.
- **Support:** best-effort founder help when capacity permits; no response-time, resolution-time, availability, or implementation commitment.

## Business-model signals

- **Model:** compound founder-funded developer framework plus a separate paid demonstration service used as proof and acquisition context.
- **Buyer/user/payer:** the developer is the framework user and adoption decision-maker; no framework payer exists. Demonstration-service users may later pay for service access, but that money is not framework revenue.
- **Value unit:** a developer completes setup and one declared capability check for a named product need; repeat use or an attributable retain decision is adoption.
- **Cost/capacity:** first experiment is three weeks, at most six founder hours per week and 18 total, USD 0 paid media, and up to USD 200 of subsidized provider usage. SP-EV-045.
- **Material dependencies:** published MIT terms, one authoritative setup contract, showcase readiness, attribution, support boundaries, privacy/security decisions, and independent developer behavior.
