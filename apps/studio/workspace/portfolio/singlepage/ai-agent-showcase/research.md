# AI-agent demonstration service research

## Decision and scope

Assess the intended service only as a future audience and acquisition program for Code Framework. It is not a current sellable product, framework revenue, or proof of adoption.

## Audience and use situation

The exact service user and any paying segment remain unselected. The proposed job is to make a real multi-module behavior observable, then provide one attributable bridge to the framework evaluation path. Service engagement without that transition does not test framework demand.

## Alternatives and competition

Alternatives include direct documentation, a repository walkthrough, a sample application, a technical article, a live demo without accounts, or no showcase. AI assistants and composed agent products are category context, but no comparison currently establishes a differentiated service offer.

## Price, channels, and evidence

Token sales or paid-chat access are possible later, with no selected terms or verified demand. YouTube is the proposed first route; other channels are deferred. The service is not assembled, externally verified, or instrumented, and provider cost, privacy, AI authorization, incident handling, and support capacity remain constraints.

## Findings and unknowns

- The service can support acquisition only when its behavior, framework identity, bridge, and attribution are observable.
- Usage, payment, attention, framework evaluation, successful use, and adoption require separate events.
- No evidence currently selects a paying segment, price, support promise, or product lifecycle.
- If readiness cannot be bounded, a direct GitHub evaluation test is the defined fallback.
