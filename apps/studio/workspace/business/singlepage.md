| Offer unit         | Free evaluation of one pinned version against one named product need, followed by setup and one declared capability check                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Price/revenue      | Framework price and revenue are zero; founder funds development. The intended MIT License is selected but not yet published                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Value hypothesis   | Hypothesis: a reusable foundation spanning those domains—including a distinct ecommerce contour for product catalogs, categories, stores, attributes, cart/order state, and links to billing currencies/payment intents, files, and site widgets—can reduce repeated assembly. The 2026-08-14 local inventory records 16 modules, 156 model/relation entities, and 1,836 declared frontend variants (24 covered by Studio examples); ecommerce contributes 7 models and 19 relations. These are presence counts, not proof of complete integration, runtime readiness, compatibility, time saved, or outcomes |
| Success unit       | Setup passed plus one relevant capability check; adoption requires repeat use or an attributable decision to retain the framework                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Acquisition bridge | Founder-led walkthrough → demonstration-service explanation → tracked GitHub evaluation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

Its users, payments, runtime, and load are service signals, never framework revenue or adoption                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Capacity           | First experiment: three weeks, six founder hours per week, 18 total, USD 0 paid media, USD 200 maximum subsidized provider cost                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

The model is intentionally compound: the free Code Framework seeks developer adoption, while the service is a bounded showcase and possible standalone service economy. Their ledgers and claims remain separate. SP-EV-075; SP-EV-045.

## Lead, payment, and delivery rules

- Framework evaluation has no checkout or payment. GitHub is the delivery destination; a pinned version, current license, prerequisites, setup check, limitations, and capability check must agree.
- Demonstration-service promotional use is capped at USD 1 actual provider cost per user per day and USD 200 aggregate. No recipient-count or per-recipient-day quota is preselected. Unused subsidy may continue after the three-week measurement window, but the cash and founder-time ceilings do not increase.
- A future paid subscriber may incur up to USD 3 provider cost per day; this is not a selected customer price, margin, demand signal, or current implementation fact.
- Founder support is best effort through messenger-linked CRM visibility. It creates no SLA, availability, unlimited help, implementation work, or post-budget promise.
- Payment, subsidy, service usage, repository attention, evaluation, successful use, and adoption are recorded as different events.

## Promises and constraints

Supported now: the repository, dated inventory, current license state, selected future MIT direction, and a bounded evaluation proposal. Do not promise complete integration, production readiness, compatibility, security, easy setup, time saved, external adoption, or a live showcase until verified.

Material unresolved operating facts are: exact service eligibility and payment terms; privacy/retention and AI authority; day-reset and reconciliation rules; continuing support capacity; and owners for incident, security, refund/reversal, and ongoing operation. These remain readiness gates for external service use, not invented business facts.
