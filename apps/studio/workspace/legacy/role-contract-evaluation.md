# Role contract evaluation

This is a contract-level decision audit, not a claim about model benchmarking.
Each case asks what the instructions require the agent to decide or escalate.
The compact contract is compared with:

- the nearest retired role summarized in
  `apps/studio/workspace/legacy/legacy-agency.md` (`legacy`); the
  legacy set has no dedicated Business Analyst, Market Researcher, Brand
  Designer, or Web Designer, so the closest overlapping role is named below;
- a `generalist` given the artifact request and repository context but no
  profession contract or on-demand method.

An instruction is retained only when it adds an observable decision,
classification, threshold, or handoff while avoiding character performance.

## Normal founder cases

| Role                     | Case                                                                                   | Compact contract                                                                                                                                 | Legacy comparison                                                                                                                                   | Generalist comparison                                                                                            | Retained value                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Account Manager          | Founder has a rough offer, price, referrals, one listing, photos, and no formal brand. | Classifies facts, claims, constraints, assets, rights, goals, and highest-impact unknowns before handoff.                                        | Account Manager collects broad business and creative inputs but also chooses message, emotion, funnel, and channels too early.                      | Likely summarizes the meeting, but has no required evidence classes, asset-rights check, or usability threshold. | Prevents discovery from silently becoming strategy.                            |
| Business Analyst         | The current service is understood informally by the founder.                           | Maps acquisition through follow-up with owner, timing, system, failure, and fallback, then checks offer feasibility.                             | No dedicated role; legacy Account Manager describes a sales funnel but not the delivery and recovery process.                                       | Likely describes a business model or happy path without requiring ownership and failure handling.                | Makes website promises traceable to operations.                                |
| Market Researcher        | A few local competitors and marketplace listings are known.                            | Frames a decision, compares direct competitors, substitutes, informal solutions, and doing nothing, with source limitations.                     | No dedicated research role; legacy Analyst measures campaigns after launch and Marketing Director assumes available data.                           | Likely returns a competitor list without a comparison-set rule or observation/inference boundary.                | Reduces false confidence from sparse public evidence.                          |
| Strategist               | Several audiences, offers, and channels appear plausible.                              | Chooses one audience and situation, records rejected options, and defines one bounded experiment with positive and negative decisions.           | Marketing Director produces five acquisition chains; Project Manager expands them into tests, increasing scope before evidence supports it.         | Likely recommends several sensible tactics without a minimum signal or consequence.                              | Converts options into a focused, reversible commercial choice.                 |
| Communication Strategist | The offer needs a message and tone for the selected audience.                          | Defines promise, benefits, proof, objections, observable voice rules, prohibited claims, and an operationally valid CTA.                         | Content Architect defines funnel messages and text formulas but optimizes first for reach and engagement and does not require claim classification. | Likely writes polished copy or adjectives without a reusable claim/voice contract.                               | Makes final copy consistent, provable, and actionable.                         |
| Brand Designer           | The client has only a name, phone number, and modest source assets.                    | Selects a visual territory, writes normative tokens and do/don't rules, and produces or briefs wordmark, favicon, avatar, and Open Graph asset.  | No dedicated role; Content Architect mentions visual identity but owns no concrete identity outputs or asset provenance.                            | Likely proposes a mood, palette, and fonts without normative tokens, required outputs, or rights records.        | Produces reusable identity decisions rather than inspiration alone.            |
| Web Designer             | Approved business, strategy, message, and identity must become a site decision.        | Specifies visitor path, final copy, responsive hierarchy, form, consent, success state, receiver, response expectation, and static compositions. | No corresponding legacy role or artifact.                                                                                                           | Likely creates a section outline and sample copy while leaving conversion operations and states implicit.        | Gives engineering a complete decision package without entering implementation. |

## Red-flag cases

| Role                     | Case                                                                                                        | Compact contract                                                                                                                          | Legacy comparison                                                                                       | Generalist comparison                                                                  | Retained value                                               |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Account Manager          | “Make it premium”; audience, proof, decision authority, and image rights are unclear.                       | Escalates the ambiguity and records unknowns; does not invent a creative specification.                                                   | Is expected to define message, emotion, and style, so the vague request can become premature direction. | May translate “premium” into familiar visual clichés.                                  | Adds a hard boundary between aspiration and evidence.        |
| Business Analyst         | Nobody owns lead response, qualification, pricing exceptions, or failed delivery.                           | Marks the process unusable and routes blocking questions upstream.                                                                        | Funnel ownership stops at messaging and sales stages; fulfillment failure is not required.              | May draw a polished happy-path flow and hide the missing owners.                       | Stops design from masking an inoperable service.             |
| Market Researcher        | Founder says “everyone is the customer” and “there are no competitors.”                                     | Requires contrary evidence, alternatives, geography, confidence, and limitations; rejects representative claims from convenience samples. | Legacy roles say decisions are data-based but define no source or sampling discipline before launch.    | May accept the premise or provide unsourced market generalities.                       | Adds evidence thresholds and explicit uncertainty.           |
| Strategist               | Founder asks for every segment, five channels, and immediate scale.                                         | Rejects non-choice, selects one bounded test, and requires a threshold that changes the next decision.                                    | Five acquisition chains are a prescribed output, reinforcing premature breadth.                         | May compromise with a phased multi-channel roadmap rather than force a first decision. | Protects learning value and scarce capacity.                 |
| Communication Strategist | Unsupported superlatives, manufactured urgency, or fabricated testimonials are requested.                   | Prohibits the claims and routes evidence gaps; CTA cannot promise an unavailable response.                                                | Content rules require facts but contain no explicit evidence classes or testimonial boundary.           | May soften the wording while retaining the unsupported implication.                    | Makes claim safety an operational rule.                      |
| Brand Designer           | Generated images are requested as completed client work, team, or premises.                                 | Registers them as generated with prohibited use and refuses to treat them as proof.                                                       | No asset-evidence boundary or provenance requirement exists.                                            | May generate visually convincing material without persistent use limits.               | Keeps identity assets separate from business evidence.       |
| Web Designer             | A brochure page is requested while lead receiver, SLA, price path, consent, and fulfillment are unresolved. | Escalates missing operations and refuses to call the design implementation-ready.                                                         | No legacy website owner checks these dependencies.                                                      | May finish an attractive page and generic contact form.                                | Makes conversion design accountable to the business process. |

## External library comparison

- Domain Experts contributes the compact core plus on-demand references pattern
  (MIT): https://domainexperts.dev/.
- Agency Agents contributes selected operational checks, but long character
  framing, invented experience, and verbose output style are not retained (MIT):
  https://github.com/msitarzewski/agency-agents.
- `wshobson/agents` supports separating roles, skills, and commands and loading
  detailed expertise only when needed (MIT):
  https://github.com/wshobson/agents.
