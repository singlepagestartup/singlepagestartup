# Acquisition channel selection

Load this reference only when `strategy.md` needs a first traffic source or an
acquisition creative needs channel constraints. Platform rules and formats
change; verify current availability, policy, targeting, dimensions, cost, and
analytics before making a live recommendation.

## Select by job, not popularity

| Channel family                                                  | Useful when                                                         | Typical role in the path                         | Primary risk to check                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Search advertising (Yandex Direct, Google Ads)                  | Buyers express an existing, specific need                           | Intent capture → landing page → lead/purchase    | Demand volume, query ambiguity, tracking, landing-page fit |
| Visual discovery (Pinterest)                                    | Appearance, planning, or inspiration precedes purchase              | Search/discovery → saved reference → site        | Asset production, geographic fit, delayed conversion       |
| Short video (VK Clips, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels) | The offer can be demonstrated quickly and repeated                  | Algorithmic reach → profile/site/channel         | Production cadence, weak intent, rights, platform access   |
| Long video (YouTube)                                            | Trust requires demonstration, explanation, or comparison            | Search/recommendation → deeper proof → site      | Production cost, time to signal, expert availability       |
| Professional publishing (Habr, LinkedIn, VC.ru, Medium)         | Expertise and a considered B2B decision matter                      | Article/post → authority → conversation/site     | Editorial fit, proof depth, long feedback loop             |
| Community discussion (Reddit, Discord)                          | The audience already gathers around a problem or practice           | Participation → trust → opt-in/conversation      | Community rules, self-promotion, moderation capacity       |
| Owned follow-up (email, Telegram, WhatsApp)                     | Permission and repeated contact already exist                       | Opt-in → nurture/service communication → action  | Consent, deliverability, moderation/support workload       |
| Social community (VK, Instagram, Odnoklassniki, X)              | Audience-platform fit and ongoing publishing capacity are evidenced | Reach → profile/community → direct response/site | Algorithm dependence, content capacity, regional access    |
| Paid social (VK Ads, Meta Ads, myTarget)                        | Targeting and creative can test a specific audience/offer           | Paid reach → landing/native lead                 | Policy/access, tracking, creative fatigue, minimum budget  |
| Newsletter platform (Substack)                                  | Recurring expertise is itself valuable and publishable              | Publication → subscriber relationship → offer    | Writing cadence, audience portability, regional payment    |

## First-channel decision

Compare options against audience presence, buying intent, credible creative the
business can produce, time to useful signal, budget, geography, platform access,
operational follow-up, and reversibility. Select one traffic source for the first
experiment. Record rejected options and the evidence that would justify adding
one later.

Do not carry forward the former rule to create five acquisition chains. The
compact strategy keeps the useful reach → follow-up → conversion reasoning but
tests the smallest chain that can change a decision.
