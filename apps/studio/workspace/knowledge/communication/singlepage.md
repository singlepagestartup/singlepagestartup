# Communication and content decisions

Load this reference when `brand.md`, `website.md`, or an acquisition creative
needs a reusable content rule.

## Content unit

For each unit define audience situation, one intended decision, primary claim,
proof, useful detail, objection, action, channel constraint, and prohibited
implication. Adapt the structure to the job:

- discovery: earn attention by naming a recognizable problem or outcome;
- explanation: make the offer, process, price principle, and trade-off clear;
- proof: show attributable evidence without turning generated material into a
  case or testimonial;
- objection handling: answer a real decision barrier with evidence or an
  explicit limitation;
- action: promise only the next step the business can deliver.

The former content system's useful hook, main point, proof, call-to-action,
format, and acceptance-criteria ideas are retained. Fixed publishing ratios,
large content calendars, platform recipes, and post-launch performance loops are
not loaded by default because they do not improve the current website decision.

## Review

Reject content that optimizes reach while obscuring the offer, uses engagement
as proof of demand, repeats unsupported superlatives, fabricates authority, or
routes people to an unowned follow-up step. Verify current platform constraints
at use time instead of treating legacy dimensions or algorithm advice as facts.
